Here’s a clear, step-by-step guide to building your custom SmartLine 360 production line in Factory I/O, using the components available in your version. We’ll build the scene in three distinct stations.

📋 Step 0: Setup

  1. Open Factory I/O.

  2. Go to File → New (or press Ctrl+N) to create an empty scene-53.

  3. Make sure you are in Edit Mode. If you see a play button on the toolbar, you are in Edit Mode. The Palette should be visible on the right side of the screen. If not, click on the Palette icon to show it-53.

🏗️ Station 1: Infeed & Color Sorting

This station detects items and sorts them by color.

Step 1: Build the Main Conveyor Line

  1. From the Heavy Load Parts category in the Palette, drag and drop a Roller Conveyor (2m) into the scene-53.

  2. This is your main infeed conveyor. You can right-click on it and select Rotate (or use the Y key) to change its direction if needed.

Step 2: Add Item Detection

  1. From the Sensors category, drag a Diffuse Sensor to the edge of the conveyor-53. This sensor will detect when an item arrives.

  2. Place the sensor near the start of the conveyor.

Step 3: Add Color Detection

  1. From the Sensors category, drag a Vision Sensor and place it after the Diffuse Sensor-11.

  2. Right-click on the Vision Sensor and select Configuration.

  3. In the configuration window, you can select which colors the sensor should detect (e.g., Red, Blue, Green). This is how the PLC will know the color of each item-.

Step 4: Build the Diverting Mechanism

  1. From the Operators category, drag a Pusher and place it across from the Vision Sensor-. The Pusher will act as your diverting actuator.

  2. Position it so that when it extends, it pushes items off the main conveyor.

  3. From the Heavy Load Parts category, drag two more Roller Conveyors. Place them perpendicular to the main conveyor, on the side where the Pusher will push items. These are your diverted lines for different colors.

Step 5: Test Your Layout

Switch to Run Mode (press F5) to see if items move along the conveyor and are detected. If you see a red box around any parts, they are intersecting, and you'll need to adjust their positions before they will work in Run Mode-53.

🏗️ Station 2: Assembly Station

This station simulates picking up parts and assembling them.

Step 1: Use the Pick and Place as Your Core Component

  1. From the Stations category, drag a Pick and Place (2-axis or 3-axis manipulator) into the scene-.

  2. This station will serve multiple purposes:

    • It can simulate the Vibrating Bowl Feeders by picking parts from a conveyor or a designated pickup point.

    • It can simulate the Assembly Press by picking up a lid and placing it onto a base-.

    • It can simulate the Palletizer later on by picking up boxes and stacking them-.

Step 2: Set Up the Assembly Sequence

  1. You will need to program the Pick and Place's sequence in your PLC logic. The sequence will be:

    • Pick up a part (e.g., a base from a feeder location).

    • Pick up another part (e.g., a lid).

    • Assemble them by placing the lid onto the base.

Step 3: Add Quality Control

  1. From the Sensors category, drag another Vision Sensor.

  2. Place it after the Pick and Place station. This sensor will be used as your Quality Sensor.

  3. In its configuration, you can define what a "good" assembly looks like versus a "bad" one (e.g., by detecting the presence of the lid on the base).

Step 4: Add Reject Mechanism

  1. From the Operators category, drag another Pusher.

  2. Place it after the Quality Sensor. This will act as your Reject Actuator. It will push faulty items off the line.

🏗️ Station 3: Packaging & Palletizing

This station packages the final product and prepares it for shipping.

Step 1: Use the Elevator for Packaging

  1. From the Stations category, drag an Elevator into the scene-.

  2. This station will act as your Stackable Box Elevator. It will collect items into a Stackable Box.

  3. Right-click on the Elevator and place a Stackable Box in its collection area.

Step 2: Use the Pick and Place for Palletizing

  1. Drag another Pick and Place station into the scene.

  2. Position it near the Elevator. This will act as your Palletizer, moving filled boxes onto a pallet.

  3. From the Items category, drag a Pallet and place it next to the Pick and Place-53.

Step 3: Connect the Stations

  1. Use additional Roller Conveyors to connect the output of Station 2 (Assembly) to the input of the Elevator in Station 3.

🔗 Mapping the I/O

  1. Go to File → I/O to see the Tags (I/O points) that your scene has created.

  2. Each part (conveyor, sensor, pusher, etc.) will have a Tag associated with it. In the I/O window, you'll see these tags with names like Conveyor1, VisionSensor1, Pusher1, etc.

  3. You can rename these tags for clarity (e.g., rename VisionSensor1 to ColorSensor). In the I/O window, double-click the tag name to change it-.

  4. Copy the tag names. You will need these exact names when you create your PLC program in TIA Portal to control the scene-.

🧠 Next Steps

  • PLC Programming: Your next step is to open TIA Portal, create a new project, and add a PLC (e.g., S7-1200). You'll create a new tag table and add tags that match the ones from your Factory I/O I/O window-11.

  • Connectivity: Configure the communication driver in Factory I/O (File → Drivers) to connect to your PLC simulation (e.g., PLCSIM or Modbus TCP/IP)--5.

  • Download and Test: Download your PLC program and then click Run in Factory I/O to test your entire production line.

This custom scene will be the foundation of your SmartLine 360 project and will provide the necessary complexity for your complete OT-IT data pipeline.
